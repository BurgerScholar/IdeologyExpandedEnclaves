# Design Document

## Vision
Make ideology matter outside the player's colony through persistent world-map communities with their own people, beliefs, needs, relationships, and history.

## Core Loop
1. Discover an enclave.
2. Inspect its identity and population.
3. Travel there by caravan.
4. Visit, trade, donate, recruit, convert, defend, or raid.
5. Gain rewards and consequences.
6. Return later to a changed community.

## First Enclave: Pilgrim Camp
- Generated name
- Generated leader
- Population of 6–12
- Neutral faction
- Central campfire
- Basic sleeping area
- Food and medicine
- Future trade and recruitment
