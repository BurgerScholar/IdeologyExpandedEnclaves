# Ideology Expanded: Enclaves

Bring ideology beyond your colony.

Discover named ideological communities on the world map, travel to them by caravan, meet their people, and eventually trade, recruit, donate, convert, defend, or raid.

## Planned Features
- Visit ideology-based enclaves
- Trade with specialized communities
- Recruit believers through gameplay
- Donate food, medicine, and silver
- Build reputation
- Attend rituals
- Raid hostile communities
- Watch enclaves grow, decline, convert, or disappear

## Status
Early alpha. Core world spawning, caravan travel, generated encounter maps, persistent identities, and neutral populations are working.

## Requirements
- RimWorld 1.6
- Ideology DLC
